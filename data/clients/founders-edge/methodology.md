## Core Problem

**Name:** Lots of activity, not enough profit

**Definition:** Trade and construction business owners put in enormous effort but get ordinary results because the business was built by default. It has no strategic master plan. Every new dollar of revenue costs almost as much effort as the last. The business grows proportionally to the owner's hours rather than compounding through applied strategy.

**Why it persists:** The owner has never mapped where they want to go or the specific leverage points that get them there faster with less. Without a strategic master plan built around exponential leverage, more effort produces more of the same.

## Three Named Enemies

### Enemy 1: The Accountability Coach
**Description:** A business coach who provides support, goal-setting, and weekly check-ins but no strategic leverage plan. They keep you accountable to doing more of the same thing.
**Redirect line:** "I don't understand how coaches can coach without doing the strategic plan first. Support and accountability without strategy is staying busy in the wrong direction."

### Enemy 2: Common Wisdom
**Description:** Taking strategic cues from peers and industry contacts who are in the same situation. The echo chamber where everyone is struggling and confirming each other's excuses. "It's tough times." "You can't grow in this economy."
**Redirect line:** "The laws of business are immutable. They don't change with the economy. The question is whether you've built a business that applies those laws or one that just reacts to conditions."

### Enemy 3: Learning From Your Own Mistakes
**Description:** Building a business through trial and error rather than following proven strategies. The solo experimenter paying full tuition for lessons someone else has already learned.
**Redirect line:** "There is R&D, rip off and duplicate. You don't have to make every mistake yourself. The fastest way to where you want to be is a proven recipe, not improvisation."

## Four-Phase Process: The Profit Freedom Formula

### Phase 1: Foundations (first 60 days)
**What happens:** Discovery of goals and financial reality. VIP welcome call, Goals and Objectives call (the Five Wants), Revenue Resource Map prep with Finance Guru, DISC Leadership Discovery, Half-Day 9-Point Strategy Intensive.
**Deliverable:** The Revenue Resource Map, a proprietary 10-year financial model mapping what the client needs to do across all growth levers to reach their magic numbers.

### Phase 2: Quick Wins, Power Moves and ROI
**What happens:** From the Revenue Resource Map, the team identifies the highest-leverage actions for the first 30, 60, and 90 days. KPI Dashboard and Scorecard built. Sales and pipeline tracker set up. Rolling cashflow tool built.
**Deliverable:** The KPI Dashboard and Scorecard, 4 to 6 metrics the client tracks every month. Plus a confirmed first-90-day action plan.

### Phase 3: Fast-Growth Rhythm (12 months)
**What happens:** Monthly Momentum Sessions (group, 3 hours, strategy plus accountability). Monthly Leadership Mentoring Sessions (mindset and leadership capacity). Quarterly Strategy Intensive days (review, reset, realign).
**Deliverable:** A running Revenue Resource Map updated quarterly, plus a leadership profile that grows with the business.

### Phase 4: Annual Expansion
**What happens:** Two-day in-person Annual Strategy Intensive. Reviews the full year, maps the next two. Celebrates wins. Resets the five-year and ten-year picture.
**Deliverable:** Updated five-to-ten-year strategic plan. New goals and objectives set.

## Top Strategies

### 1. The Revenue Resource Map
**How it works:** A proprietary financial model that maps where the client is now, where they want to be (magic number), and the exact resources, hires, and strategy shifts needed to close the gap. Built with the Finance Guru from P&L and balance sheet data.
**Ideal fit:** Any trade or construction business owner who has never had a strategic financial model, which is most of them.
**Concrete outcome:** An $8M glazing company discovers it needs two additional project managers, specific margin targets per job type, and a pipeline tracker. Revenue pathway to $14M mapped within the first 60 days.

### 2. The Five Wants
**How it works:** A goal-setting framework that defines the five things a founder actually wants from the business: magic number (income or profit target), magic lifestyle, size and scope, give back and contribution, and magic valuation at exit. These numbers become the North Star for the Revenue Resource Map.
**Ideal fit:** Founders who are building toward something but have never articulated it clearly enough to plan around.
**Concrete outcome:** "Once we do the goal setting objectives call, they've got a lot more certainty of what they want."

### 3. The 9-Point Strategy Intensive
**How it works:** A half-day session with Ryll, her expert team, and other ambitious business owners. Reviews the nine critical areas of business growth: strategy, marketing, sales, operations, IT, recruitment, branding, leadership, and financial management. Maps which levers produce the highest return.
**Ideal fit:** Business owners who are good at delivery but have never stepped back to look at the health of all nine areas simultaneously.
**Concrete outcome:** Annie, a painting company owner, identified one strategy from this session. Forward work jumped from $150,000 to $500,000 in the first month.

### 4. KPI Dashboards and Scorecards
**How it works:** Four to six key metrics selected per business, tracked monthly. Gives the owner the exact number they need to move each week to stay on track with the Revenue Resource Map. Named the "F.O.O.", focus on outcomes.
**Ideal fit:** Owners who are data-averse or overwhelmed by too many numbers. The discipline is narrowing down to the vital few.
**Concrete outcome:** "It's the stats or the story. You've always got one of them. With the dashboard, you've always got both."

### 5. The Monthly Momentum and Leadership Rhythm
**How it works:** Two monthly group sessions, one on business strategy (Momentum), one on personal leadership growth. You can never be more than 30 days off track. Fast30 coaching sessions available on demand for urgent issues.
**Ideal fit:** Founders who get stuck in the weeds and drift from their strategic plan between check-ins.
**Concrete outcome:** Tyrone, a trade business owner, had the full program investment returned in two weeks through a single strategy shift identified in his first Momentum session.

## Objection Library

All pairs are verbatim from Ryll's sales calls.

**1. "Have you worked with businesses in my industry before?"**
"That's interesting. I used to get asked when I was 21 and started in this business if I'd done this business or that type of business, and at 21, I'd done none of them. I haven't done any of your industry specifically, and what I've found over all the years is business is immutable. The laws of business are immutable. It doesn't matter what you're actually selling."

**2. "It sounds daunting. I'm worried we're not up to it."**
"I've had one client in the last 5 years where this entire process did not work for them. And it's not about whether you're up for it. My process makes sure that the plan, the numbers we come to in the Revenue Resource Map, they're your numbers. I'm just facilitating getting you to your magic number, your magic profit. They're absolutely yours and you will own them."

**3. "We were too flat out last year. We couldn't have committed to anything."**
"When you help someone get what they want, you get what you want. Part of what we do is we build out the Revenue Resource Map, so we figure out when the right time is, and we build around your reality, not a generic schedule."

**4. "I need a few months to sort out our systems and structures first."**
"It makes sense why you're saying that. But it generally doesn't work that way. It's better to start. The first 60 to 90 days, we're working directly with you on what's actually going on in your business. You can join the group stuff whenever you want. Some people want to dive straight in. Others just want to focus on themselves for the first 60 days."

**5. "What happens if we're not getting results after 3 months?"**
"We can never be more than 30 days off track. We meet every single month. You can't get the bright shiny object distraction factor either. If there's a problem becoming obvious, you book what we call a Fast30, a focused 30-minute session to get back on course."

**6. "I've used a business coach before and it was just support calls."**
"Yeah, support and accountability versus strategy. I don't really understand how coaches can coach without doing the strategic plan first. I need help to say here is where your business is, and here is where it can be, and here is A, B, C, D to get there. That's the strategy piece. That's what makes the difference."

**7. "I need to run this past my business partner first."**
"I was assuming that would be the case. What I can do is send you the recording of this session, plus the strategic support agreement. That captures everything we just looked at in document form. And then let's book a follow-up call together once they've had a chance to review it."

**8. "We've got some serious debt right now."**
"I've been bankrupt. I definitely would not be thinking about the word liquidation for these numbers. These are quite normal. All we have to do is help you sort it out, make a plan, get everyone to stop calling you, and push the revenue. Sales solves everything, in brackets, at the right margin."

**9. "I can't pay today, I'll have the money on Tuesday."**
"Absolutely. You can give me the details and I'll process it on Tuesday. What I'd like to do is book in your welcome call right now, so you're away. Not technically supposed to book you until you've paid, but I'm booking you in because I know you're going to take care of it. Congratulations."

**10. "I don't want to bite off more than I can chew. How much time does this actually take?"**
"Fair enough. In terms of time for you, a welcome call, a goals session, about 45 minutes with our Finance Guru, two half-days or a full day together in the first 4 to 6 weeks, and another hour to finalise the plan. Really, it's about a day and a half of your time to get the whole foundation built. Everything else fits around your schedule."

**11. "The investment is at the higher end, I need to think about it."**
"There are three levels people come in at. One is for under a million in revenue. The next is where you are, revenue and profitability at this level. I'll share both so you can see which fits and we can move from there."

**12. "I've tried to build the systems myself but I never have time."**
"We do the heavy lifting for you. You're part of it but you don't carry it. We support you and you just track the progress."

**13. "I'm worried about the commitment level given what's already on our plate."**
"The first 60 to 90 days can go fast, done in 30 days for some, 60 for most, a bit longer for others. It depends entirely on your time pressure. And you choose which pieces to activate first. We'll leave the optional pieces until you're ready."

**14. "You don't know our industry."**
"The laws of business are immutable. They don't change with industry. The specific strategies shift, but the principles of leverage, of working backwards from your magic number, of knowing your nine key growth areas, those are universal. I've applied them across hundreds of sectors."

**15. "Will this work if we're already doing internal change projects?"**
"The program is designed to complement what you're already building, not compete with it. What we find is the Revenue Resource Map actually gives you a framework to prioritise which change projects to do first, the ones that move the numbers the most."

**16. "I just need to let my finance person know before committing."**
"Of course. I'll send you the Freedom Growth Partnership agreement, that captures everything we just walked through. And I'll also send through the invoice with your choice of monthly or annual payment. Book in a quick follow-up call with your finance person when they've had a look."

**17. "Past coaching experiences didn't deliver results."**
"Yeah. And I can tell from how you describe it, it was support and accountability, not strategy. You can't coach without a strategic plan. You can't tell someone to work harder if you haven't first mapped the fastest, easiest, most profitable path to where they want to go. That's the difference."
