# Founder's Edge HQ: Voice Corrections

## Purpose

This file grows over time. When Ryll or John corrects a piece of content Irene produces, the before and after are logged here with a note on what changed. The corrections build a fingerprint of Ryll's exact voice that sharpens Irene's output over time.

---

## Factual sourcing rule (pre-draft, not post-draft) — applies to ALL content including paid ad scripts

Before drafting any content that includes client results, revenue numbers, named client stories, or statistics, check:
1. `~/source-materials/` — verified source documents (webinar transcripts, testimonial tracker, sales call transcripts).
2. `references/verified-client-results.md` in the `client-methodology` skill — pre-catalogued confirmed results from Ryll's own words.

Do not open a draft with a constructed client scenario even if it fits the niche description perfectly. Constructing plausible-sounding results from methodology description text is fabrication. John Anderson will catch it. Source first, draft second.

Incident (June 2026): A LinkedIn post opened with a concreting owner doing $4.5M, not a real client, constructed from the niche description. It passed all voice and anti-slop checks. Factual verification is a separate, earlier step.
